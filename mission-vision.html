Mission & Vision

What we are working towards, how we get there, and the values that shape every engagement.

Our Vision
To be Papua New Guinea’s leading technical experts in home-grown policy solutions that measurably improve the livelihoods of Papua New Guineans.
Our Mission
To bring Papua New Guinean expertise to the design, delivery and evaluation of development initiatives — working alongside government, donors, communities and the private sector to turn evidence into practical action.
Our Motto

Delivering with HEART

Five values that drive how we operate and how we work with our clients.

H

Honesty

We tell clients what we find, including when it is not what they hoped to hear.

E

Excellence

Technical work that stands up to scrutiny, delivered on the terms we agreed.

A

Accountability

We own our commitments and our mistakes, to our clients and to the communities our work affects.

R

Respect

For the people, land and knowledge systems of Papua New Guinea, and for every partner we work alongside.

T

Teamwork

Different disciplines, one table. No problem is handed to a single specialist and left there.
