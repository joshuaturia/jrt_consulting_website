About Us

A wholly owned Papua New Guinean company providing consulting services to special projects across government, donor partners, non-government organisations, the private sector and community development programmes.

Who we are

Papua New Guinean expertise, for Papua New Guinea

JRT Consulting Limited is a wholly owned Papua New Guinean company, registered with the Registrar of Companies under the 1997 Companies Act and established in December 2021. We provide consulting services to special projects across government, donor partners, non-government organisations, the private sector and community development programmes.

Why we exist

Papua New Guinea presents challenges and opportunities that are not well served by solutions designed elsewhere. We believe the knowledge needed to improve the livelihoods and wellbeing of Papua New Guineans is best created and applied by Papua New Guineans — and that doing so well takes a team that can hold several disciplines at once.

A firm of mixed backgrounds

JRT is a family firm, and deliberately not a firm of specialists in one thing. A forester with four decades in the public service, a development practitioner working across public policy and migration, and a geospatial scientist working in remote sensing and climate data are the three directors. That mix means a forestry question is also examined as a policy question and a data question before we answer it.

What we do

Services across development, policy and natural resources

01

Policy, Planning & Governance

Designing and reviewing policy that works in a Papua New Guinean context, and helping institutions plan and govern their delivery of it.

02

Monitoring, Evaluation & Research

Evidence about whether programmes are working, and research that stands up when the findings are inconvenient.
